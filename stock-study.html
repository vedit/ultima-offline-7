The murder scene

The UO corpse-carving implementation places a torso, head, both arms, both legs and blood in the world. The floor victim uses opposing stock limb facings, rotated into the spread pose, with dyed cloth remnants. Its recipe lists each sprite in draw order with counterclockwise rotation in degrees. Rotate each cropped piece before placing its center. Frame 32 transposes the finished composition to follow the U7 reflection. A pitchfork pins Inamo to the stable wall above a pool of blood. Other frames use a human or creature death pose, with skin tone and worn equipment chosen per frame. Pixel offsets are measured from each cropped sprite’s center, positive right and down, relative to the scene anchor.
