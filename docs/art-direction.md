# Art direction

The original Ultima Online assets define the camera, perspective, lighting,
scale and placement conventions for every asset in this project. Ultima VII
defines the identity, material, colours and recognizable design of the object.

- Choose the closest stock UO reference before making or importing art. Match
  its projection and visible faces in ground, wall, inventory, paperdoll and
  animated views. Do not transfer a U7 view angle directly into a UO view.
- In paperdolls, preserve the stock weapon's grip, finger clearance and hand
  angle. Daggers follow the stock dagger; swords follow the stock longsword.
  Other families need their own UO reference. Ground and equipped art share a
  design, but use the perspective appropriate to each view.
- Follow UO's upper-left lighting, material shading, pixel density and contact
  points. Review at native size as well as enlarged. Geometry and placement
  must support the art: furniture feet touch the floor, props rest on surfaces,
  and walls, corners and roofs meet.
- Every generated ground item needs a visible, thin contour at native size.
  Use dark material-colored edges, including blade tips and hand guards;
  preserve openings between guard bars and split blades. Check on light and
  dark floors. Paperdoll artwork follows its own stock UO edge treatment.
- Across the entire armory, leather, scale and plate chest pieces own their
  matching shoulder protection and armlets/arm plates. This includes antique
  and magic variants. Attach them in both paperdoll and world rendering; they
  are not separate inventory items. Gauntlets end at the wrists. Ground chest
  icons need not display the attached arms. Chain already includes sleeves.
- Apply these defaults from the first design, including future equipment.
  Show original U7, ground and equipped comparisons before installing revised
  artwork in the normal game. Identify asset composites separately from live
  captures. A prior approval does not approve an unseen revision.

These rules record the user's armory and perspective decisions of 2026-11-14.
